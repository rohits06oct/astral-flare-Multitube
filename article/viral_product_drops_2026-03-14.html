These "sparks" are designed to build hype and identify the most engaged audience members. If you are not monitoring these specific channels as they breathe, you are already behind the "FOMO" (Fear Of Missing Out) curve. Tracking the teaser phase is where the advantage is won or lost.

To truly understand if a product is worth the investment—or the effort of the secondary market—one must be able to monitor the diverse expert opinions and community reactions simultaneously.

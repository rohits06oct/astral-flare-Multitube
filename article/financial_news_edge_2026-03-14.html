Every speech by a central bank official, every minute detail in a "dot plot" projection, and every decimal point in an inflation report can trigger massive surges or sell-offs across multiple asset classes. For the sophisticated observer, monitoring these official briefings in their raw, unaltered form—rather than waiting for a news summary—is a critical part of the process.

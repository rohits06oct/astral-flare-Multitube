The Flash Crash Phenomenon: Why Seconds Matter

The "Flash Crash" has become a recurring character in the story of modern markets. These sudden, intense periods of volatility are often triggered by a single piece of viral information that is picked up and acted upon by high-frequency trading (HFT) algorithms within milliseconds. Whether it’s a misinterpreted social media post from a major CEO or a leaked internal memo, the initial "spark" of a market movement is often small but incredibly potent. If you are not monitoring the specific sources of these sparks in real-time, you are essentially flying blind.

This "human-in-the-loop" monitoring—paired with raw data—is the only way to distinguish between a genuine market shift and a temporary algorithmic blip.

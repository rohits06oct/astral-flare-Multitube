Top 10 Chrome Extensions for Video Multitasking and Productivity

By YoutubeMulti Pro Team February 21, 2026

Enhance your multiview experience with these essential Chrome extensions designed for power users who live in the video ecosystem.

  • Video Speed Controller: Essential for breezing through slow segments across all your screens.
  • Picture-in-Picture (PiP): For when you need that one critical video to follow you across tabs.
  • Ublock Origin: Keeps your embeds clean and fast by removing intrusive scripts.
  • Dark Reader: Protects your eyes while you monitor your video wall late at night.

Combining these extensions with a dedicated player like YoutubeMulti Pro creates the ultimate 2026 creator workstation.

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: April 11, 2026

Cookie Statement: YoutubeMulti.online does not store any cookies related to personal use for general visitors. We are committed to a tracker-free experience for our regular users. We only store essential data for premium customers who have purchased our product to maintain their session and subscription status.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer by websites that you visit. They are widely used in order to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use a limited number of cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: Only for premium users to recognize them and keep them signed in.
  • Preference Settings: To remember choices you make (such as layout settings) for the session.
  • Performance: We use Google Analytics to understand site traffic and usage patterns.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Our advertising partners, such as Clickadilla and Google Adsense, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting your browser's ad settings.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to refuse to accept cookies and to delete cookies. The methods for doing so vary from browser to browser, and from version to version.

5. More Information

If you have any further questions about our use of cookies, please contact us on our Contact Page.

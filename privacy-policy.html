Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 11, 2026

Privacy Commitment: YoutubeMulti.online does not store any cookies related to personal use for general visitors. We respect your anonymity. We only store data for premium customers who have purchased our product to manage their subscription and provide premium features.

1. Information Collection

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from you unless you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact forms or during the subscription process.

2. Use of Information

Any information collected is used solely to:

  • Provide and maintain our service.
  • Notify you about changes to our service.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Gather analysis or valuable information so that we can improve our service.

3. Data Storage

For premium users, we store minimum necessary data (such as email and subscription status) to ensure access to paid features. General users can browse and use the tool without any personal data being recorded on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Google AdSense

We use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense on our website. These third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Security

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Page.

7. Privacy & Data FAQ

Is my multi-stream layout saved?

Session-based layout choices are stored in your browser's local storage to ensure a consistent experience during your visit. This data never leaves your device unless you are a logged-in premium user.

Does YoutubeMulti record which videos I watch?

No. We provide the player infrastructure, but we do not track or log the specific video URLs you input into your multi-screen grids.

Can I request my data to be deleted?

Premium users can request account and data deletion at any time by contacting our support team. Free users have no server-side data to delete.

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from you unless you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact forms or during the subscription process.

Any information collected is used solely to:

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Page.
